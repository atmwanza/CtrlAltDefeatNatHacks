    Finger tapping

    MDS-UPDRS 3.4, the clinician's test for slowness of movement (bradykinesia)

    1. Rest your forearm on the table with your hand on the mark, facing the camera.
    2. At GO, tap your index finger on your thumb as fast and as big as you can, opening wide from the very first tap.
    3. Keep tapping until the bar finishes (10 seconds). Then do the other hand.
