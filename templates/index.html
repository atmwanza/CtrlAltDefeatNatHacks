  3. Once you reach the finish ring, tap Analyze Spiral.

    Finger tapping

    MDS-UPDRS 3.4, the clinician's test for slowness of movement (bradykinesia)

    Prototype · published reference
    Camera preview appears here
    1. Rest your forearm on the table with your hand on the mark, facing the camera.
    2. At GO, tap your index finger on your thumb as fast and as big as you can, opening wide from the very first tap.
    3. Keep tapping until the bar finishes (10 seconds). Then do the other hand.
    Compared with 47 healthy people and 53 people with Parkinson's (Kasemsap et al., PLOS ONE 2025; data CC BY 4.0). Severity band benchmarked against 489 neurologist-rated videos (Islam et al., npj Digital Medicine 2023; MIT). Separate from the spiral result. A screening estimate, not a diagnosis.
